Essay Coaching

Writing a good application essay is hard; you want to make sure it's clear and easy to read, that your voice is coming through, and that you stand out, with it all crammed into a word limit that really should be bigger. The best way to improve your writing is to have someone else read it just like a reviewer would–-and that’s where I come in!

What I provide

I provide editing assistance and serve as a writing coach for hire, with a particular focus on application materials for undergraduate institutions, scholarships and fellowships, summer research programs, and biomedical graduate programs. I am happy to read and provide feedback on any academic writing; please reach out below for a consultation!

Qualifications

For every application essay I’ve submitted, I’ve edited several more for friends and colleagues. I have successfully applied for and received the following:

  • The Brown Fellows scholarship, a full-ride scholarship to Centre College in Kentucky
  • The Goldwater Scholarship, a nationally competitive award given to only ~450 students a year pursuing a PhD in a STEM field
  • The Mayo Clinic Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship, admitted twice in back-to back summers
  • The Mayo Clinic Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences, where I am currently pursuing my PhD in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology

Why hire help?/h2>

Nothing makes an essay better than a second set of eyes. I read the whole essay carefully and provide detailed feedback and comments, plus an overall summary of its strengths and weaknesses. I can also schedule zoom consultations to talk through an essay directly and answer any questions directly.
